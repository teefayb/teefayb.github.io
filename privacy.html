Privacy Policy for teefayb publisher

Effective date: 2026-10-18

What teefayb publisher is

teefayb publisher is a personal tool run by the owner of the teefayb YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only to publish and manage videos on that one channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

teefayb publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using teefayb publisher, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, teefayb publisher accesses the owner's own YouTube channel only, in order to:

  • upload videos;
  • manage videos and their details (for example titles, descriptions, thumbnails and visibility);
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for the channel.

It does not access any other channel, any other person's account, or any Google data beyond what is listed above.

How information is stored

  • The access and refresh tokens Google issues are stored only in the owner's local macOS Keychain. They are never sent to any server other than Google's, and are never shared.
  • Video files, video details and analytics that the tool reads are used only by the owner, on the owner's own computer, to publish videos and review the channel's performance.
  • The tool does not use cookies, does not run trackers or analytics of its own, and does not show advertisements or third-party content.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. There are no other users, and no data is passed to third parties, apart from the requests the tool sends to Google's YouTube APIs to do the tasks listed above.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens stop working. They can also be removed from the macOS Keychain.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or complaints about privacy: teefaybgaming@gmail.com.
